What COVER24 will do

COVER24 is currently in development. These are the planned features — everything a manager needs to run the schedule, nothing they don't.

Employee list

Keep a simple, up-to-date list of everyone on the team, ready to assign to shifts.

Shift creation

Assign an employee to a date, start time, and end time in a few clicks.

Calendar view

See the full weekly or monthly schedule across the whole team at a glance.

Double-booking protection

COVER24 won't let you schedule the same employee for overlapping shifts.

Employee shift view

Employees can check their own upcoming shifts without calling the manager.

Shift offers

Send a shift to an employee and track whether it is accepted, cancelled, or still pending.

Analytics

See hours worked per employee at a glance, broken down by month.
